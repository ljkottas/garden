GARDEN GROUP STUDY

Learn together. Grow in confidence. Help others begin.

An eight-week, Scripture-centered study to help ordinary Christians become more intentional, prepared, and faithful in relational evangelism.

8 weeksFree for churches and small groupsNo teaching experience required

A study designed to be shared

Each session begins with Scripture. Participants read the lesson aloud together, pause for discussion and reflection, and practice using GARDEN with real relationships in mind. The goal is not to finish a course but to develop a lasting habit of thoughtful, faithful engagement.

Participant Guide

Full read-aloud curriculum, discussion prompts, practical exercises, prayer, and a course-long Stage Three preparedness project.

Leader’s Guide

The same curriculum with additional facilitation notes, theological guardrails, and help guiding discussions. No lecture required.

Eight weekly sessions

Each week builds toward being prepared to understand, question, explain, respond, and recommend resources on one real topic.

How to lead a GARDEN group

Gather a few Christians who want to grow. Work through one session each week. Take turns reading sections aloud, stop at the discussion and reflection prompts, and pray together. The Leader’s Guide helps someone facilitate rather than deliver a lecture. In Week 8, participants are invited to consider helping another group begin.

Use and sharing: Churches and small groups are welcome to use the study without registration. Please retain GARDEN attribution when sharing the materials. We'd also welcome feedback from groups who use it.
