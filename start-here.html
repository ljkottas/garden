Your first steps

Start small. Start with someone you already know.

GARDEN is not asking you to become an expert before you begin. It is asking you to become intentional: one person, one prayer, one conversation, one thoughtful next step.

Walk the path ↓Learn the framework
1
Choose one person.Someone already present in your life.
2
Begin praying.For the person, not merely the conversation.
3
Pay attention.Listen for beliefs, questions, experiences, and openings.
4
Take one next step.A question, follow-up, resource, or another conversation.

You do not need to have an evangelism “event.”

The mission field is already woven into ordinary life—family, work, neighborhoods, hobbies, friendships, school activities, and the people you see repeatedly. GARDEN begins there.

A simple first-week plan.

No curriculum. No giant reading list. Just begin paying closer attention to one person and one relationship.

1
Write down one name.Choose someone you genuinely know and care about.
2
Pray for them each day.Pray for their life, needs, relationships, questions, and openness to God.
3
Listen differently.Notice what they say about meaning, morality, suffering, God, religion, truth, and their own story.
4
Do not force anything.If a natural opening appears, ask a thoughtful question. If it does not, keep building the relationship.
5
Capture the next step.Afterward, record what you learned and decide whether there is something worth researching or revisiting.
Keep the goal clear

This is not about becoming an evangelism machine.

GARDEN is not…

  • Turning people into projects.
  • Forcing spiritual conversations.
  • Having an answer for everything.
  • Winning arguments.
  • Trying to manufacture conversion.

GARDEN is…

  • Caring about people intentionally.
  • Being prepared when opportunities arise.
  • Asking thoughtful questions.
  • Growing in your ability to give an answer.
  • Trusting God with what you cannot control.
