The non-negotiable

AI is the assistant. It is not the authority.

These frameworks are designed to help you think, research, compare, organize, and prepare. They do not replace Scripture, careful interpretation, trusted scholarship, prayer, or wise Christian judgment.

1

Use AI to slow down thinking.

Ask it to clarify claims, identify assumptions, separate issues, and represent opposing views fairly before you respond.

2

Use AI to prepare, not posture.

The goal is not to sound smarter in the next conversation. It is to understand the issue well enough to engage another person honestly and helpfully.

3

Verify what matters.

Important factual, historical, biblical, and theological claims should be checked against reliable sources rather than accepted because an AI produced them.

Two different tasks

Choose the framework that matches the conversation.

The External and Internal frameworks share a commitment to careful reasoning, but they are solving different problems and therefore follow different paths.

The key distinction

External and internal dialogue require different kinds of care.

External: explain and compare worldviews.

The question is often whether Christianity makes better sense of reality than the competing explanation.

  • Understand the objection before answering it.
  • Expose assumptions without caricature.
  • Compare explanatory power.
  • Choose a response that fits the person and situation.

Internal: interpret faithfully within Christian boundaries.

The question is often how Christians who affirm Scripture and historic orthodoxy should understand a disputed issue.

  • Exegetically first.
  • Theologically second.
  • Philosophically third.
  • Distinguish essential doctrine from legitimate disagreement.

AI belongs mainly in the quiet space between conversations.

It is especially useful during Reflect & Research—after a question arises and before you return to the person with a thoughtful next step.

Keep the hierarchy clear

Use technology without outsourcing judgment.

The framework should make you more thoughtful and responsible, not more dependent on whatever an AI happens to produce.

Scripture over output.
AI does not determine Christian doctrine.
Sources over confidence.
Fluent wording is not evidence.
People over winning.
The human being in front of you matters more than the argument.
