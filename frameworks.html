Guided AI for Christian learning

Use AI with guardrails.

AI can be an extraordinary learning assistant, but it should not become your authority. GARDEN provides two different master frameworks that shape how AI reasons, what theological boundaries it respects, and how it helps you prepare for real human conversations.

Choose a framework ↓Search the Research Library
The principle

AI is the assistant. It is not the authority.

Use AI to clarify, compare, question, and learn. Verify important claims against Scripture and trusted sources, and bring what you learn back into real relationships.

Powerful tools are most useful when you know what they are for—and what they are not.

These frameworks are designed to guide study, not replace Scripture, the local church, pastoral wisdom, or careful human judgment.

How to use them

Attach. Ask. Learn. Verify. Return.

Attach

Start a new AI conversation and attach the appropriate master framework.

Ask

Enter the real question, objection, passage, doctrine, or statement you want to explore.

Learn

Use the response to deepen your own understanding rather than merely copy an answer.

Verify & Return

Check important claims and bring what you learned back into Scripture, church, and real conversation.

The goal

Better preparation should lead to better human engagement.

Use AI to reduce the pressure of not knowing—not to avoid the people, relationships, churches, and conversations that Christian growth and evangelism actually require.
