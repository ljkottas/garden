Many Christians care about evangelism—but still feel unprepared when the moment comes.

GARDEN is built for that gap. You do not need to become an expert before you begin. You can start with no knowledge and little confidence, then grow into someone who can ask better questions, find good answers, and engage thoughtfully over time.

The long game

Evangelism can unfold across months or years.

Someone may raise an objection at work, mention a painful experience with church, ask what you think happens after death, or casually say science has made belief in God unnecessary.

You may not know what to say. That is not the end of the conversation. It can be the beginning of preparation.

Later you can return naturally: “I was thinking about what you said the other day…”

You can begin before you feel ready

Preparedness grows in stages.

Stage One is a legitimate starting point. It just should not become a permanent destination.

Stage One

No knowledge and no comfort. Listen, admit what you do not know, and commit to thinking about it.

“That’s a good question. I’m not sure how I’d answer that right now. Let me think about it and get back to you.”

Stage Two

Growing comfort and curiosity. Use questions to clarify claims, test assumptions, and keep the burden of proof where it belongs.

“What do you mean by that?” “How did you come to that conclusion?”

Stage Three

Knowledge and confidence together. Combine strategic questions with direct explanation, correction, evidence, and worldview analysis.

The goal is not omniscience. It is steady growth toward thoughtful Christian engagement.
